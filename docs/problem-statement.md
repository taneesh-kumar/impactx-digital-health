## Foundation Scope Notice

The exact solution scope, user journeys, clinical domain areas, and specific feature specifications are being finalized and will be documented in subsequent milestones.

At this foundation stage:
- No specific disease, condition, clinical workflow, or patient demographic has been assumed or hardcoded.
- No third-party health API (e.g., FHIR, EHR integrations) has been integrated.
- The focus is solely on providing an extensible, robust technical foundation (Next.js, NestJS, Prisma, PostgreSQL).
